# Temporary questions while reading replies

Date: 2026-11-11
Type: Added
Scope: web, server, core
PR: [#9](https://github.com/liuneng1994/penguin-harness/pull/9)

[中文](2026-11-11-chat-btw.zh.md)

Select text in a completed assistant reply and choose **btw Ask** to open a temporary conversation beside the passage. Each reply also has a btw action for questions about the whole reply. On phones, the conversation opens at the bottom of the screen.

The original selection stays highlighted while you ask and follow up. You can stop generation, copy an answer, and scroll the main conversation with the question panel open. Closing the panel, pressing Escape, or leaving the Session clears the temporary conversation.

btw uses the Session's model and a read-only snapshot of its current context. Its questions and answers are held in page memory and do not enter the main conversation or Trace. Model usage is included in the normal usage totals.
